About

Support first. Cloud next. Community always.

I’m a detail-oriented technical support engineer with hands-on experience in SaaS product support and a growing practice in AWS and Google Cloud. At IDeaS Revenue Solutions I help global hospitality clients get unstuck: installations, process monitoring, application and integration issues, and the unglamorous work of reading logs until the system is stable again.

I’m an AWS re/Start graduate, an AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner and AI Practitioner, and a Google Associate Cloud Engineer. Day to day that looks like bridging customer problems with cloud fundamentals — EC2, S3, IAM, VPC, and the operational habits that keep environments predictable.

Outside work I volunteer with AWS User Group Pune and show up for Pune’s builder rooms: Datadog Live, OpenSearch, Cloud Native, and hands-on AI build days. In 2026 I also ran an internal AI agents workshop during AI Week at IDeaS and took first place in our AI & Automation initiative.
